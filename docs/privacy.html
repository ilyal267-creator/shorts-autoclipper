Privacy Policy

Shorts Autoclipper · Effective 13 September 2026 · Terms of Service

Shorts Autoclipper ("the app") is a self-hosted tool that turns a long video into short vertical clips and uploads them to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts that its operator has connected. It runs on the operator's own computer. There is no hosted service, no user sign-up and no database run by us.

What the app accesses

When you connect an account, the platform shows you exactly which permissions the app asks for. The app uses them only for the purposes below.

  • TikTok — your basic profile (an account identifier, display name and avatar) to confirm which account is connected, and permission to post videos to that account.
  • Instagram — your basic professional-account profile and permission to publish Reels to that account.
  • YouTube — permission to upload videos to your channel.

The app does not read your messages, followers, comments, analytics or other people's content.

Where your data goes

  • Sign-in tokens issued by TikTok, Meta and Google are stored in files on the operator's computer. They are sent only back to the platform that issued them.
  • Source videos and rendered clips stay on the operator's computer. A finished clip, with its title, caption and hashtags, is sent only to the platform it is posted to.
  • Transcripts: speech is transcribed on the operator's computer. The transcript text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to choose clips and write captions. The video itself is not sent.
  • Narration: when AI voiceover is turned on, the narration script is sent to ElevenLabs to generate the voice.

Anthropic and ElevenLabs handle that data under their own privacy policies. We do not sell data, show ads, or use analytics, tracking or cookies. That includes this page.

How long it is kept

Clips, transcripts and logs stay on the operator's computer until the operator deletes them. Sign-in tokens are kept until you disconnect the app or they expire. Anything already posted is controlled by the platform and your account settings there.

Disconnecting

You can revoke access at any time:

  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions
  • Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites
  • Google/YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions

Once access is revoked, the stored token stops working. You can also ask the operator to delete the token file.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube account you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.
